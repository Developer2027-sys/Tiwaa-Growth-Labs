A buyer fills your form at 10:40 at night. If the first reply comes the next afternoon, they have probably already chatted with two other projects. WhatsApp auto-replies let you respond in seconds, any time, with exactly what the buyer asked for.

Here is how we set up WhatsApp automation for real estate so every enquiry gets a fast, helpful reply and serious buyers reach your sales team quickly.

Why reply speed matters so much

Property buyers rarely look at one project. They click ads, fill two or three forms and wait to see who responds. A quick reply with the brochure and price shows you are organised and serious. A slow reply sends them to the developer who answered first.

Our target for the first WhatsApp reply on every new enquiry, day or night. Measure your current average for a week to see where you stand.

Step 1: Send a useful first reply

The first message should arrive within a minute and give the buyer something they asked for, not just "thanks, we'll call you". Include the buyer's name, the project, the brochure and price sheet, and one simple question.

Hi Rahul 👋 Thanks for your interest in Sector 65 Residences. Here is the brochure and price sheet 📄 Which home are you looking for? Reply 1 for 2 BHK, 2 for 3 BHK, 3 for 4 BHK. 2

Step 2: Ask two or three qualifying questions

Use quick-reply buttons so buyers can answer with one tap. Budget range, configuration and buying timeline are enough. If your Meta lead form already asked these, skip them and move straight to the visit.

Step 3: Offer site-visit slots

Offer two or three specific slots instead of "when are you free?". Specific choices get faster answers. Once a buyer picks a slot, confirm it with the address, a map pin and the name of the person who will meet them.

Step 4: Hand over to a person quickly

The moment a buyer books a visit, asks about price negotiation or types a detailed question, a salesperson should take over. With LeadsPulse CRM, the chat is assigned to the right person with a WhatsApp alert and a one-tap call button, so the buyer never feels stuck with a bot.

Step 5: Reminders and follow-ups

  • Visit reminders the day before and two hours before, with the map pin.
  • A gentle nudge if the buyer stops replying, after a day and again after three days.
  • A thank-you after the visit with the floor plan they liked and the next step.

Rules to follow

  • Use the official WhatsApp Business API, not unofficial bulk tools that can get your number blocked.
  • Get opt-in. Your lead form or website should say that the buyer agrees to be contacted on WhatsApp.
  • Use approved templates for messages sent more than 24 hours after the buyer's last reply.
  • Make it easy to stop. Respect buyers who reply "stop" or ask not to be contacted.

How to set it up

You need a WhatsApp Business API account, a verified business profile, message templates and a connection between your lead sources and the chat. Connect your landing page, Meta and Google lead forms so every enquiry triggers the first reply automatically. Then send chats and site visits back to your ad platforms with the Conversions API, so ads learn which leads actually visit.

Frequently asked questions

Will buyers feel they are talking to a bot?

Not if the first reply is useful and a person takes over quickly. Automate the brochure, questions and slots, and hand serious buyers to a salesperson within minutes.

Do I need the WhatsApp Business API?

For automated replies at scale, yes. The free WhatsApp Business app is fine for a few chats a day, but it cannot connect to lead forms, a CRM or multiple salespeople reliably.

Can I send messages to buyers who enquired weeks ago?

Only with approved message templates and if they agreed to be contacted on WhatsApp. Messages outside 24 hours of the buyer's last reply must use a template.

Does this work with Meta lead forms?

Yes. Leads from Facebook and Instagram instant forms can trigger the first WhatsApp reply within seconds.
