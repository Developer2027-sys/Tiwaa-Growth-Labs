Key takeaways
  • Start building EOIs 6 to 8 weeks before launch, not in launch week.
  • Give buyers a clear reason to register early: priority unit choice, a launch price or a refundable token.
  • Send every EOI to a CRM and call it the same day, then keep the list warm on WhatsApp until launch.
  • Judge the pre-launch by EOIs that convert to bookings, not by the size of the list.

The launches that sell fastest are usually decided before launch day. When a developer opens bookings with hundreds of warm, pre-qualified buyers already waiting, the first weekend sets the tone for the whole project. That list of interested buyers is your EOI list.

This guide explains how we plan pre-launch campaigns for real estate developers: when to start, what to offer, which content and ads to run, and how to turn registrations into bookings.

What an EOI list is, and why it matters

An EOI (expression of interest) is a buyer telling you, before launch, that they want to be considered for a unit. Some developers collect a refundable token amount; others simply register details and preferences. Either way, an EOI list gives you three things:

  • Demand you can measure. You see which configurations and price bands buyers want before you fix launch pricing.
  • A ready audience. Launch-day messages go to people who already know the project.
  • Momentum. A busy first weekend creates the urgency that brings the next wave of buyers.
Note

Collecting token money before RERA registration is not allowed in most states. Check with your legal team what you can promote and collect at each stage.

When to start: a 6-week pre-launch plan

Two weeks of pre-launch is rarely enough to build trust for a high-value purchase. A simple six-week plan looks like this:

  1. Weeks 1–2: Tease. Location, master plan, the developer's track record and a short teaser film. No price yet.
  2. Weeks 3–4: Open EOIs. Launch the EOI page and lead ads with the early-registration offer.
  3. Week 5: Price signal. Share an indicative price range and floor plans with registered buyers first.
  4. Week 6: Launch countdown. Site-visit invitations, launch-day details and reminders to every EOI.

Build an EOI offer buyers understand

People register early when they get something for it. The offer should be one sentence long and easy to repeat:

  • Priority unit selection: first choice of floor, view or facing.
  • Launch price: a price that rises after the first phase is sold.
  • Fully refundable token: removes the fear of committing too early.

Avoid vague offers like "exclusive benefits". If the sales team cannot explain it in one line on the phone, buyers will not understand it in an ad either.

Teaser content that builds curiosity

Pre-launch content has one job: make the right buyers curious enough to register. What works well:

  • A 30 to 45 second teaser film of the location and master plan.
  • Drone shots of the site and nearby landmarks such as schools, metro stations and highways.
  • Reels on the developer's delivered projects, to build trust.
  • A countdown to the date when price and floor plans are shared with EOI holders.

The EOI landing page

Send ad traffic to a dedicated page, not your homepage. A good EOI landing page has:

  • The offer and the launch date above the fold.
  • A short form: name, phone, configuration and budget range.
  • Location map, master plan, the developer's track record and RERA details (once registered).
  • A WhatsApp button for buyers who prefer to chat.

Keep it fast on mobile. Most pre-launch traffic comes from Instagram and Facebook on a phone.

Ads that bring EOIs, not just clicks

Run Meta lead ads for reach and Google Search ads for buyers already searching for new launches in your locality. Three rules keep the list high quality:

  • Qualify in the form. Ask budget and configuration, as explained in our guide to cutting cost per site visit with Meta lead forms.
  • Retarget teaser viewers. People who watched most of your teaser film are your warmest audience.
  • Send results back to Meta. With the Conversions API, Meta learns which registrations become site visits and finds more people like them.

Bring channel partners in early

Channel partners often hold relationships with your best buyers. Give them a clear pre-launch kit, a separate tracked form or code, and a simple rule for who owns which lead. That way their EOIs and yours sit in the same list without disputes later.

Keep the list warm until launch

An EOI collected six weeks before launch goes cold if nobody talks to it. Plan a light, regular rhythm:

Hi Neha, thanks for registering for Sector 65 Residences. Here is the master plan and location video 🎬 Floor plans for the 3 BHK are ready. As an EOI holder you get them a week before launch 📐 Launch is on 12 January. Would you like a site visit slot on Saturday the 14th? Yes, please book 11 AM

Set these up with WhatsApp automation, and keep every EOI in a CRM such as LeadsPulse so your team can see who has opened, replied and visited.

Convert EOIs on launch day

On launch day, EOI holders should feel like insiders. Give them a dedicated time slot or priority window, a named contact in the sales team and a simple way to block their unit. Track how many EOIs turn into site visits and bookings, and compare channels. That number, not the size of the list, tells you which pre-launch activity worked.

Frequently asked questions

How many EOIs should I aim for before launch?

It depends on the number of units in the first phase and your price band. Many developers aim for two to three EOIs for every unit they plan to release, since not every registration converts to a booking.

Can I collect money for EOIs before RERA registration?

In most states, developers cannot advertise or accept bookings before RERA registration. Rules differ, so confirm with your legal team what you can promote and collect at each stage.

How long before launch should I start marketing?

Six to eight weeks is a good starting point for most residential launches. Larger or luxury projects often need longer to build trust.

What is a good EOI to booking conversion?

It varies widely by project, pricing and how well the list is nurtured. Track it for every launch so you can compare channels and improve the next pre-launch.
