How we work: brief to booked site visits

Five steps, agreed with you up front, so you always know what is happening this week and what comes next.

  1. Week 1

    Study

    We study your project, pricing, micro-market and competing launches.

  2. Week 1–2

    Plan

    Channels, budget, and lead and site-visit targets you sign off.

  3. Week 2–3

    Create

    Walkthrough film, Reels, creatives, landing page and WhatsApp replies.

  4. Week 3–4

    Launch

    Ads go live and every lead reaches your team in seconds.

  5. Every week

    Improve

    Leads tracked to site visits; budget moves to what works.
