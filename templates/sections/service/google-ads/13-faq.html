How much should I spend on Google Ads?

It depends on the project, its price band and micro-market. Most developers and channel partners start between ₹30,000 and ₹1,00,000 a month in ad spend. We share a keyword and budget plan with expected leads before you commit.

How soon will I see results?

Search campaigns can bring enquiries within days of going live. Costs usually settle and improve over the first 6–8 weeks as we clean search terms and the bidding learns.

Is the ad spend included in your fee?

No. Ad spend is paid directly to Google from your own account. Our management fee is separate, so you always see exactly what Google charged.

Do I own the Google Ads account?

Yes. Campaigns run in your account and you keep full access to it, the data and the tracking, even if you stop working with us.

Search, Performance Max or YouTube: which do I need?

Most projects start with Search and local ads, which catch people already looking. Performance Max, Demand Gen and YouTube are added once tracking is proven and we know what converts.

Can you fix my existing account?

Yes. We start with a free audit that shows wasted spend, tracking gaps and quick wins, then restructure without losing your account history.

Do you make the landing pages too?

Yes. Our team builds fast landing pages for each campaign, or improves your existing pages for better conversion.

How do you report results?

You get a live dashboard and a monthly plain-language report on leads, cost per lead, site visits, bookings and what we changed, plus a monthly review call.
