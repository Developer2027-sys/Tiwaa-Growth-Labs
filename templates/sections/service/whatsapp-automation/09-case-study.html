From 6-hour replies to 45 seconds

How a Gurugram real-estate channel partner stopped losing leads after office hours. Sample case for preview

CP
Real-estate channel partnerGurugram · 40+ projects · 12 sales agents

The challenge

Over 300 leads a week from Meta and property portals, answered manually from personal phones. Night and weekend leads waited hours, and no one knew which agent spoke to whom.

What we did

  • Moved to the official WhatsApp Business API on Tiwaa.io
  • Built a bot that shares brochures and qualifies by budget
  • Auto-assigned hot leads to agents via LeadsPulse
  • Weekly broadcasts for new launches and price updates
