What is the WhatsApp Business API?

It is Meta's official way for businesses to use WhatsApp at scale: chatbots, broadcasts, a shared inbox and integrations on one verified number, without the limits of the normal WhatsApp Business app.

Will my number get banned for broadcasts?

Not when you use the official API with opted-in contacts and approved templates. That is exactly how we set it up on Tiwaa.io.

What does it cost?

Tiwaa.io plans start from ₹999/month. Meta also charges small per-conversation fees for marketing and utility messages. Our setup and management fee is quoted after a free consultation.

Can I keep my existing number?

Yes, in most cases your current business number can move to the API. It just can no longer be used in the normal WhatsApp app at the same time.

Can the bot reply in Hindi?

Yes. Flows can be in English, Hindi, Hinglish or any regional language your customers prefer.

Can a human take over the chat?

Anytime. Your team sees every conversation in the shared inbox and can step in with one click.

How long does setup take?

Most clients are live within 7 days, depending on how quickly Meta business verification completes.

Does it work with my CRM?

Yes. We connect LeadsPulse, Zoho, HubSpot, Google Sheets and most CRMs, so every chat and lead status is recorded.
