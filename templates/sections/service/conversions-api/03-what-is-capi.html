CAPI explained simply

What is the Conversions API?

No jargon. Scroll through five steps and watch what happens when a buyer fills your form.

✉️
The pixel is like a letter sent through a crowded market

It travels through the buyer's phone and browser. Ad blockers and privacy settings can stop it on the way.

vs
📞
CAPI is like calling Meta directly from your office

Your own server tells Meta and Google what happened. Nothing in the buyer's browser can block it.

01

A buyer fills your form

Someone sees your Instagram ad for a 3 BHK and fills in the enquiry form. Now Meta needs to know that this ad worked, so it can show it to more people like this buyer.

The pixel sends a message from the buyer's browser
02

Some messages never arrive

The pixel travels through the buyer's phone. Ad blockers, iPhone privacy settings and cookie limits stop some of these messages, so Meta thinks your ad brought fewer leads than it really did.

Ad blockers and privacy settings stop some messages
03

CAPI sends it directly

With CAPI, your own server tells Meta and Google about every lead. It is a direct, secure line. Phone numbers and emails are scrambled (hashed) before sending, so personal data stays private.

Your server tells Meta directly: every lead arrives
04

It also reports what happens later

The real results, a call, a site visit, a booking, happen in your sales office, not on the website. The pixel can never see them. CAPI sends them from your CRM the moment your team updates a lead.

Site visits and bookings are sent from your CRM
05

Meta learns who really buys

Now Meta knows which people visited and booked, not just who filled a form. It shows your ads to more people like them, so the same budget brings more site visits.

Meta finds more people like your real buyers
