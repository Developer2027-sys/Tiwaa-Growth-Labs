SEO, explained simply

Six terms worth knowing

No jargon in our reports. Here is what the main terms mean for your business.

Technical SEO
Fixing how your site is built (speed, mobile, indexing) so Google can read and rank it.
On-page SEO
Making each page's content, titles and headings match what buyers search for.
Local SEO
Showing up in Google Maps and "near me" results for your city or area.
Backlinks
Links to your site from other trusted websites. Google treats them as votes of confidence.
Core Web Vitals
Google's measures of how fast and stable your pages feel to real visitors.
GEO (AI search optimisation)
Making your brand appear and get cited in AI answers like Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT.
