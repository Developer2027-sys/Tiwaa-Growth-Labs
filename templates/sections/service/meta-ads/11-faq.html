How much should I spend on Meta ads?

Most clients start between ₹30,000 and ₹1,50,000 a month in ad spend. We recommend a budget after reviewing your goals and market, and our management fee is quoted separately.

How soon will I get leads?

Campaigns usually go live within 7 days, and most clients see their first leads in the first week. Costs typically improve over the first 4–6 weeks as we test and optimise.

Why are Meta leads often low quality, and how do you fix it?

Easy forms attract casual clicks. We add qualifying questions, filter fake numbers, reply instantly on WhatsApp and feed lead quality back to Meta so it finds better people.

Do you make the ad creatives?

Yes. Our in-house team makes Reels, carousels, statics and copy, and refreshes them every week. You approve everything before it runs.

Facebook or Instagram, which is better?

We usually run both from one Meta campaign and let results decide. Instagram Reels tends to work well for younger and visual products, Facebook for wider age groups.

Who owns the ad account and data?

You do. Campaigns run in your own Meta Business account, and you keep full access to ads, audiences and data.

Can you connect leads to my CRM?

Yes. Leads flow into LeadsPulse or your existing CRM in real time, with WhatsApp alerts to your sales team.

Do I need a lock-in contract?

No. We start with a 3-month plan, because Meta campaigns need a few weeks to learn and settle.
