Do I need a CRM if I already use Excel?

If you get more than a few leads a day, yes. Sheets cannot assign leads, send reminders or show which ads bring sales. A CRM does all three and stops leads slipping through.

Which CRM do you recommend?

For most Indian sales teams we set up LeadsPulse, our own CRM. If you already use Zoho, HubSpot or Salesforce, we set that up properly instead.

What exactly do you track?

Website forms, calls, WhatsApp clicks and chats, site-visit bookings, brochure downloads and key page actions, along with the source, campaign and keyword behind each one.

Can you track phone calls from ads?

Yes. Call tracking shows which ad, keyword or page brought each call, and calls can be counted as conversions in Google Ads.

How long does setup take?

Tracking and a CRM are usually live in about two weeks, depending on how many lead sources and team members you have.

Will my team actually use it?

We keep stages simple, set it up on their phones with WhatsApp alerts and train them. Managers get a usage report so nothing is skipped.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Your data stays in your own accounts, access is role-based and you can export everything at any time.

Do you work with other agencies running our ads?

Yes. Many clients use us only for tracking and CRM, so every agency and channel is measured the same fair way.
