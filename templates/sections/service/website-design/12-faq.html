How long does a website take?

A landing page takes about a week. Most business websites take around three weeks, and online stores four to six weeks, depending on pages and content.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the number of pages, features and whether it is a store or custom build. We share a fixed quote after a short call, with no hidden costs.

Will I be able to update it myself?

Yes. We build on easy platforms like WordPress, Shopify or Webflow and show your team how to change text, photos and blogs.

Do I own the website?

Yes. The domain, design, content and code are yours. You get all logins and can move it anywhere.

Will it work well on mobile?

Every site is designed mobile-first and tested on real phones, tablets and laptops before launch.

Is SEO included?

Basic SEO is: fast loading, clean structure, titles, meta tags, schema, sitemap and Search Console. Ongoing SEO is a separate service.

Can you redesign my current site without losing rankings?

Yes. We keep your best pages, map old links to new ones and check Search Console after launch, so rankings are protected.

Do you provide hosting and maintenance?

Yes. Our care plan covers fast hosting, SSL, daily backups, security updates and small monthly edits.
